Returns & Warranty Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Before you buy

Every PC listed is a used/refurbished, hand-built machine. It's fully tested and confirmed working before it's listed for sale. You're welcome — and encouraged — to inspect and test the PC in person at pickup before the sale is finalised. Once you've checked it over and taken it home, the sale is complete.

What the warranty covers

Each build comes with a warranty period stated on the product listing (3, 6, or 12 months — check the specific listing or your invoice). During this period, the warranty covers:

  • Genuine hardware faults arising from normal use (e.g. a component failing on its own)
  • Parts not performing as described at the time of sale

What isn't covered

The warranty does not cover:

  • Software issues — operating system problems, driver conflicts, viruses/malware, or anything caused by software you install
  • Physical damage — drops, spills, liquid damage, or damage from moving/transporting the PC
  • Misuse — overclocking beyond safe limits, unauthorised modifications, opening/tampering with components, inadequate ventilation, or use outside normal conditions
  • Normal wear and tear (used builds may show minor cosmetic wear — this is expected and disclosed at time of sale, not a fault)
  • Change of mind — we don't offer refunds or returns simply because you've changed your mind after taking the PC home. Please make sure to fully inspect and test the build at pickup.

How to make a warranty claim

If something covered by warranty goes wrong within your warranty period:

  • Message us on Facebook/Messenger or via the Contact page
  • Include your invoice number (found on your invoice, format INV-XXXXXXXX) and a description of the issue
  • We'll get back to you to arrange next steps — this may involve diagnosing the issue remotely first, or bringing the PC back for inspection

If a fault is confirmed to be covered by warranty, we'll repair or replace the faulty part at no extra cost. If a fault turns out to be outside the warranty (see exclusions above), we'll let you know before doing any paid repair work, so you can decide how you'd like to proceed.

Pickup & order changes

Placing an order on this site reserves a build for you — it doesn't finalise the sale. Full payment and handover happen in person at pickup. Need to cancel or reschedule a reserved order? Message us on Messenger as soon as you can.

Questions

Anything not covered here, or unsure if your situation qualifies? Reach out via our Contact page — happy to help sort it out.
